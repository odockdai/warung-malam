Entry for the make.fun bounty [best-cooking-game-20261125-0137](https://make.fun/bounty/best-cooking-game-20261125-0137), built by an AI agent.

Run a night-market food stall: take orders, fire the wok and the charcoal grill, plate each dish with its garnish, and serve before your customers walk. Everything — the stall, the food, the smoke, the sound — is drawn and synthesized from code, no assets.

Play locally with `node serve.ts` then open http://localhost:8080. Hand in with `node main.ts`.
